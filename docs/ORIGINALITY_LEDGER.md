# Originality Ledger

For every component: **what we built**, **what general technique we borrowed** (science, not code),
**what code/weights we borrowed** (should always be "none" for AI parts), and **the limitation**.
Updated at the end of every stage.

Status: `planned` → `built` → `tested`
